About

I am completing my Master's degree in Quantum Information and Photonics at the University of Padua, Italy. My thesis research, carried out at TU Delft, focused on photon-counting intensity interferometry using superconducting nanowire single-photon detectors (SNSPDs) and avalanche photodiodes (APDs). Throughout my studies I have gained extensive hands-on experience in quantum optics laboratories, including single-photon detection, optical alignment, interferometer stabilisation, Bell-state generation and measurement, quantum state tomography, and coincidence-based correlation measurements.

My research interests lie at the intersection of quantum optics, quantum communication, and quantum information theory. I am particularly interested in quantum correlations, entanglement-based protocols, and the characterisation of non-classical light sources.

Research Experience

Oct 2025 – Apr 2026

Photon-Counting Interferometry with SNSPDs

TU Delft — Faculty of Applied Sciences (Master's Thesis)

Independently designed and implemented a photon-counting interferometry setup for stellar intensity interferometry. Performed optical alignment, detector calibration, and timing-jitter characterisation in single-photon detection systems. Conducted photon-correlation measurements and coincidence analysis to extract second-order coherence functions g(2)(τ). Adapted Python-based pipelines for photon-stream analysis, coincidence histograms, and statistical noise modelling. Improved timing resolution by a factor of four over the course of the project.

Nov 2024 – Mar 2025

Polarisation-Drift Compensation for Fibre-Based QKD

Quantum Future Lab, University of Padua

Contributed to the design, testing, and optimisation of an automatic polarisation-drift compensation system for fibre-based quantum key distribution. Conducted stability and performance tests under varying environmental conditions for long-duration key-exchange experiments.

2024 – 2025

CV-QKD Simulation & Quantum Communication Projects

University of Padua & UPC Barcelona

Studied a Gaussian-modulated coherent-state CV-QKD simulation in Ansys Optics, performing shot-noise calibration, excess-noise analysis, and secret key rate evaluation across varying channel conditions. Conducted security and performance analysis of discrete-variable QKD protocols (3-state 1-decoy BB84), including finite-key effects, QBER, and error-correction efficiency (LDPC, CASCADE).

2023 – 2025

Laboratory Experience in Quantum Optics

University of Padua

Prepared and characterised polarisation-encoded qubits; implemented Bell-state generation and measurement with SPAD detectors, waveplates, PBS, and coincidence detection systems. Performed two-qubit quantum state tomography, reconstructing density matrices via linear inversion and MLE. Investigated optical QRNG protocols (trusted and semi-device-independent) with entropy analysis and randomness certification. Studied quantum teleportation and entanglement-based protocols for quantum communication networks.
